Slide 1: Problem & Data Structure Choice

"In high-volume restaurants, fairness and timing are everything. If orders are processed randomly, customer wait times become unpredictable and food gets cold. To solve this, we implemented a **FIFO (First-In, First-Out) Queue** data structure."

Slide 2: Architectural Flow ($O(1)$ Complexity)

"The system features two main touchpoints: the **POS Station** which enqueues incoming tickets at the rear in constant time $O(1)$, and the **Kitchen Display System (KDS)** which dequeues orders from the front as chefs complete them."

Slide 3: Live Demonstration

"Let's observe the live app: when I click 'Place Order', it joins the back of the line. Notice how the front order stays locked as the primary task. When the chef clicks 'Bump Order', it safely dequeues the oldest ticket, maintaining complete operational order."
